A bit about me.

With a passion for empowering individuals and businesses to make informed financial decisions, Jeannie is committed to providing tailored guidance and support to help clients achieve financial success.

With a heart to serve, Jeannie achieved Premier Bronze In 2021 and 2022, as well as Top Rookie Financial Consultant in 2021 in her agency. Her consistent hard work and efforts led to her promotion to Senior Financial Consultant within 8 months of joining the industry and achieved 1st Runner up in EB2Life category 2022 for Enterprise Business Solutions

Jeannie specializes in Enterprise Business Solutions for SMEs, Succession Planning and Business Continuity for business owners. With her deep understanding of the unique challenges faced by business owners, Jeannie is dedicated to helping her clients navigate through complex financial scenarios faced by businesses to provide clarity and tailored solutions for her clientele.

As a trusted financial consultant to business owners, Jeannie works closely with clients to develop customised solutions to meet their specific goals and objectives. Jeannie is a keen learner who believes in providing excellence in service. She consistently strives to improve herself to add value to her clients and people around her.
